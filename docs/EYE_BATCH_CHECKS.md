## Анимация
- **feel-1**: Start with -ObshagaDebugKeys and -LogCmds="LogObshaga Verbose". Hold Ctrl and press W: the character takes slow, half-speed steps with no sliding, and the log shows 'anim: SK_MaleAwalk'. Release W and keep holding Ctrl: the squat pose comes back ('anim: SK_MaleAcrouch'). While crouched and standing still, press L or pick up an item with E: the model does not stand up, and the log shows 'holds item: 1'. Then crouch-walk with the item: the arms keep holding it while the legs step.
- **feel-6**: Walk forward and release W: the legs stop within about 0.15 s, with no half-speed stepping on the spot. With LogObshaga Verbose, 'anim: SK_MaleAidle' follows almost at once. The first steps after pressing W do not slide. Also watch Palych patrol: he must still walk without flickering between stepping and standing.
- **progr-6**: After re-running tools/setup_looks.py, start with -ObshagaDebugKeys and press K (catch me). For the whole 8-second interrogation, the caught character stands and keeps shaking its head; it never sits in the air. Edge case: pick phrase #2 on the emote wheel, then press K at once. The head shake must keep looping and not freeze.

## Известные мелочи (не исправлены, посмотреть глазами)
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaLookComponent.cpp:176: Side effect of the feel-1/progr-1 fix: when a crouched player moves while carrying something, the item hangs about 30 cm below the hands. The upright Walk clip now plays, but CarryPoint (ObshagaCharacter.cpp:63-65) is attached to the capsule, and the crouched capsule centre sits 30 cm lower (90 -> 60). The old squat pose lowered the arms together with the item. It is cosmetic and rare: crouch has no gameplay effect beyond speed and footstep volume (grep finds IsCrouched only in ObshagaCharacter.cpp:171/188 and PlayerController.cpp:921). A proper fix needs either CarryPoint moved by HalfHeightAdjust in ObshagaCharacter.cpp (not my file) or the size-M squat-torso-over-walking-legs variant.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaLookComponent.cpp:176: Accepted trade-off of the short variant, recorded for the record: a crouch-walking character now looks like someone walking slowly upright. Its model stands taller than the crouched capsule, and the Komendant's sight traces that capsule. Other players see no sign that someone is sneaking, and while moving the player's own 'Ctrl — присесть' gives no visible squat (the squat shows only when standing still). This is the variant the audit recommends and it removes the glide. The better variant is size M (with the current single overlay the legs would sink into the floor).
- tools/setup_looks.py:26: progr-6 takes effect only after tools/setup_looks.py is re-run in the editor on the laptop. DA_Looks.uasset in the repo is an LFS pointer and still holds 'sit'. No script runs setup_looks.py automatically (checked setup.ps1, pie-check.ps1, setup.bat). The implementer already flagged this; nothing to change in code.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaCharacter.cpp:465: feel-2, guest only, minor and not fixed. When the exit arrives, ApplyHiding switches the client to MOVE_Walking while the client's pawn is still inside the BlockAll box of the furniture. The client's PhysWalking may then push the pawn out of the box on its own (the start-penetrating floor check leads to ResolvePenetration), possibly toward the back of the furniture, before the server's position correction arrives. In that case the 'still inside the bounding box' wait ends early, and for about one ping the camera can sit at a wrong spot until the correction snaps the pawn to the exit. The host is unaffected. Fixing it means holding movement or the camera until the server position arrives. That is more invasive, and the effect is a flash of about 100 ms, so it is left as is.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaCharacter.cpp:465: feel-2 edge case, not fixed. If a player hides in a different spot during the 1 s camera-restore wait (bPeekCamera is still true), the camera moves to the new spot's door but the view is not turned outward, because SetControlRotation only runs when bPeekCamera is false. This needs an ejection and a hide in an adjacent spot within one ping, so it is not worth the change.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaHUD.cpp:1300: feel-7 together with feel-11, not fixed: Palych's subtitle moved to 0.67·H, and the suspect's interrogation panel can now grow past its old 190·Scale height to fit the notice line. Their boxes overlap only when the panel's bottom passes 0.67·H − 4·Scale: there must be about three more wrapped lines than now (a two-line notice plus wrapped choice lines), and the window must be 540 px tall or less. At the usual 16:9 sizes the panel ends at 0.30·H + 190·Scale ≈ 0.652·H, under the subtitle box at about 0.663·H. I judged this too rare to justify moving things again.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaHUD.cpp:1119: Pre-existing, outside the reviewed fixes: the Friday newspaper title uses ★ (U+2605) via LOCTEXT("PaperWeekTitle", "★ {0}"). That is in the same range the project says its font cannot draw, so it may show as a box on the Friday results. I did not change it: it is outside this review's scope and I could not confirm it on a screenshot.
- Source/Obshaga/RoomVolume.cpp:308: stuck-2 residual risk: the lid's bottom is always floor + 300 (MaxWallHeight), not the measured wall top. If the real 2nd-floor walls are lower than 300 cm, a thin item that hits the lid at an angle can slip out through the slit between the wall top and the lid. This cannot be checked here because L_Obshaga.umap and greybox_overview.png are LFS pointers, and Decorate makes the same assumption. Left unchanged. Check on the laptop by throwing a note up at an angle toward a wall in rooms 201–204.
- Source/Obshaga/ObshagaHUD.cpp:619: The client and the server decide 'outside' differently. The HUD street hint treats the player as outside when GetCurrentRoom() is null, i.e. the capsule overlaps no zone box. The server's AObshagaGameMode::IsOutsideBuilding (ObshagaGameMode.cpp:102) counts a centre point within StrandedMargin (100 cm) of a zone, with no wall in between, as still inside. If any doorway or seam leaves the capsule outside every zone box, the HUD shows 'Ты снаружи. Вход — ночной выход' after 1.5 s to someone standing inside the building, and the server never returns them. I can't see the zone extents in the .umap, so I can't confirm this happens on the current map. The 1.5 s delay softens it, and the countdown line is dropped 3 s after its deadline. Possible minimal fix: in the HUD, also require that no ARoomVolume box expanded by StrandedMargin (and 50 cm in Z) contains the location. Not applied because it is unconfirmed.
- Source/Obshaga/HidingSpot.cpp:347: Two notices for one event, but this existed before the diff and was not introduced by any group. The base AHidingSpot::EjectHiddenPlayer always sends 'Ты вышел из укрытия'. So when a player comes back through the night exit, ANightExitDoor::EjectHiddenPlayer (NightExitDoor.cpp:188-207) queues 'Ты вышел из укрытия' and then 'Ты вернулся с улицы…' or 'Слишком быстро вернулся…'. A forced morning return (ForceReturn) gives three notices in a row. Left unchanged because no group touched this path.
- Source/Obshaga/KomendantAIController.cpp:819: The new message 'Палыч тебя потерял — посиди тихо' (world group) also fires when the chased player leaves unseen through the night exit, because ANightExitDoor is an AHidingSpot. The player is then on the street, and 'посиди тихо' reads a little off. Harmless and not contradictory, so not changed.
- Source/Obshaga/NightExitDoor.cpp:118: The new message 'Прыжок с балкона — не вылазка' checks only that the player has a LeftBuildingAndReturnedUnseen task row (FindRowByCondition). It does not check that the task is still in progress, so a player who already did a proper sortie earlier that night still gets the hint. Minor, so not changed.
